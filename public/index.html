    Is it really free?

    Yes. Transcription runs on your own computer, so there are no servers for us to bill you for.

    Do subtitles help SEO?

    Yes. Search engines can't watch video, but they can read captions and transcripts. Upload the SRT to YouTube and paste the TXT transcript on your page.

    Is my video private?

    Your file is processed in your browser tab and never sent anywhere. Only the AI model is downloaded, once, and then cached.

    How long can my video be?

    Long videos work, but speed depends on your device. A laptop with a modern GPU handles about an hour of audio in a few minutes.
